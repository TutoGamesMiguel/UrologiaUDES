Surgical Plannings

An interactive section dedicated to surgical plannings in urology. Explore detailed guides, step-by-step processes, and essential considerations for various urological procedures.

Introduction

Surgical planning in urology is a systematic process that involves the detailed planning of specific surgical interventions to treat urological conditions.

This process encompasses from preoperative evaluation, the selection of techniques and approaches, to the organization of resources and postoperative management, with the objective of optimizing clinical outcomes and ensuring patient safety.
